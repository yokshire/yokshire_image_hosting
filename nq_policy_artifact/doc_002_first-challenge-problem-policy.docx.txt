L0001: The 1st Challenge
L0002: Data
L0003: Results from a social survey of a certain populace (processed to be fit for the competition)
L0004: Objective
L0005: Build a quantum circuit that classifies the data into two underlying classes.
L0006: Equipment
L0007: A 1st Challenge lab dataset consisting of:
L0008: A training set, which you can use to train your quantum circuit.
L0009: A public test set, which you can freely use to test your trained circuit.
L0010: Constraints
L0011: Submissions must follow the constraints below:
L0012: Maximum number of qubits: 8
L0013: Maximum circuit depth / number of layers: 50
L0014: Maximum two-qubit gate count: 80
L0015: Allowed gates: X, Y, Z, H, S, T, RX, RY, RZ, CX, CZ
L0016: Allowed measurement scheme: 1-qubit measurement
L0017: Allowed shots: up to 1024 shots
L0018: The training data must be encoded directly to the quantum circuit with no pre-processing.
L0019: Data augmentation is not allowed.
L0020: Training must be done exclusively with the training set. No portion of the public test set may be used for training.
L0021: Leaderboard
L0022: The leaderboard server will implement your quantum circuit and classify a private, server-side test dataset. This private test dataset is not available to participants. Ranking is determined by the following metrics, in order of priority:
L0023: Priority 1: Balanced accuracy –
L0024: Higher balanced accuracy higher ranking
L0025: (This is the foremost metric. Up to the second decimal digit.)
L0026: Priority 2: Circuit depth – the no. of layers in your quantum circuit.
L0027: Lower circuit depth higher ranking
L0028: (This acts as a tiebreaker if balanced accuracy is identical.)
L0029: Priority 3: Number of two-qubit gates – the no. of two-qubit gates in your circuit.
L0030: Fewer two-qubit gates higher ranking
L0031: (This acts as a tiebreaker if the above two metrics still result in a tie.)
L0032: Priority 4: Submission time – the time when you made your submission.
L0033: Earlier submission time higher ranking
L0034: (This acts as a tiebreaker if all other metrics still result in a tie.)
L0035: Evaluation
L0036: Evaluation will proceed as follows:
L0037: A shortlist based on the final version of the leaderboard as of the 1st Challenge deadline is compiled.
L0038: The shortlisted teams are requested to share their source code and other information with the organizers.
L0039: The organizers use the shared code and information to replicate the team’s leaderboard results and check compliance with the rules.
L0040: A team is disqualified from the shortlist and the competition if:
L0041: The organizers fail to replicate the leaderboard results with the shared materials, or
L0042: A submission is not compliant with the competition rules and constraints.
L0043: If a team is disqualified, the next highest ranked team on the leaderboard is included in the shortlist.
L0044: Results replication and compliance checks are repeated until the shortlist consists of only compliant teams.
L0045: The teams on the final shortlist (valid top 10% of submissions, minimum 10%) advance to the 2nd Challenge.
L0046: Submission Format
L0047: You will submit the following on the Submission page:
L0048: classifier.qasm: Quantum circuit architecture in the OpenQASM 3.0 format.
L0049: The circuit must contain symbolic parameter placeholders.
L0050: Parameter names must follow the format: theta_0, theta_1, theta_2, ….
L0051: weights.json: Trained parameter values in the JSON format.
L0052: The evaluation server will inject these values into the submitted circuit.
L0053: Direct input on submission page:
L0054: Index of the measured qubit.
L0055: A comment on how the training data was encoded into the circuit.
L0056: Submission Limit (Daily)
L0057: Each team may submit up to 3 submissions per day (KST, UTC+9) on the leaderboard.
L0058: Code of Conduct & IP
L0059: Participants must submit original work. Plagiarism or unauthorized copying of other teams’ solutions is strictly prohibited.
L0060: Sharing or attempting to reconstruct the test data is not allowed.
L0061: By joining, participants agree that anonymized results and circuits may be used for:
L0062: Research publications
L0063: Educational materials
L0064: Future tutorial examples (Specific IP and licensing terms will be clarified in the official rules.)
